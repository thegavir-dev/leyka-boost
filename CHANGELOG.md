## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-12

First stable release.

### UTM Tracker
- Tracking UTM parameters for Leyka donations
- First touch / last touch attribution
- Donations table with filters, pagination and CSV export
- Analytics: top sources, campaigns, first→last touch paths
- UTM link generator with history

### Toolkit
- Newsletter subscription checkbox on donation forms
- Mandatory recurring payment consent checkbox

### Close Campaign
- Toggle button to open/close donation campaigns
- Activation/deactivation statistics and logging

### Core
- Unified event log with module filtering and statistics
- Module-level on/off toggles in settings
- Russian and English localization
